# Level audit

Generated by `npm run audit:levels`. All **41 shipped rooms** have legal winning routes: 30 Regular and 11 Extra hard. Generator seeds 0–999 also pass. Historical rooms in test fixtures are not counted as shipped content.

## What the checks establish

Each layout is validated and its complete winning route replayed through the actual engine. A blocked action, death, incomplete collection or unfinished exit fails the audit. Replay must not mutate the original layout.

The separate test suite exhausts the reachable state graph after 31 selected irreversible mistakes. Search limits are treated as inconclusive, never as impossibility proofs. Two additional constrained searches check that temporary tool support and upper-ball relocation cannot simply be skipped. The recorded map hashes bind these checks to the shipped layouts.

Winning routes are upper bounds. Their length and the number of interaction turns describe execution, not human difficulty. Interaction turns involve pushes, falls, arrows, plant growth, tool use, pickup/drop or sinking; a single turn may trigger several events. Longest walk is the longest consecutive run without one of those interactions in the reference route, including treasure collection and the final approach to the exit. It is a screening measure, not a difficulty score. The expanded Regular set has not undergone blind human difficulty testing. No room is advertised as unbeatable.

## Regular

The first 11 compact rooms are preserved, followed by 19 selected additions from five independent designers. Larger footprints are used where they support coupled decisions; small layouts remain throughout the set. New stable IDs keep additions separate from existing completions. Existing Extra hard IDs, best scores and custom rooms are preserved. The original maps remain only as historical engine regression fixtures.

## Extra hard

The existing two contest-selected rooms are unchanged; see their [original contest report](challenge-contest.md). New selections come from the [five-designer tournament](tournament-2026-10-24.md), with three fresh blind attempts each. Second Floor now starts one square lower, in the exact puzzle state after its original forced opening move; the rest of its layout is unchanged. A regression check binds this revision to the frozen layout. That tournament required a new room to defeat at least two of three bounded attempts. The seven rooms from Cellar Door onward come from the [second design tournament](tournament-2026-10-30.md). Every one that was played blind was solved by at least one stronger agent player, so they were selected through maintainer playtesting rather than blind failures. Agent results do not establish human difficulty.

## Generator

The editor's generator still creates connected treasure mazes, not authored logic puzzles. Testing 1000 seeds is sample coverage, not a proof of every possible seed. Arbitrarily edited maps need their own playtesting.
