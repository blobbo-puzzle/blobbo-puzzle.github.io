# Extra hard solutions — spoilers

Developer reference, excluded from the website and standalone HTML. U/D/L/R move, W waits, X drops the held tool. These are verified winning witnesses, not guaranteed shortest routes.

## 1. Secondhand Supports

Stable ID: `challenge-a2`. 15 × 13; 45 actions.

1. Move the bottom ball left to enter the central workspace. Push the first marshmallow upward twice; vacating its former support area releases the ball at (7,7) into (7,8).
2. Push the adjacent central marshmallow right to (8,6). Leave it there temporarily: the workspace must be entered from below now and crossed westward later. Do not clear space by shoving the eastern marshmallow into the lower arrow-release alcove.
3. Reach (10,9), then retreat upward to release the left-arrow along the bottom. The ball moved at the start now redirects that arrow into (5,9), reopening the southeast approach.
4. Use the newly open lower route to collect the southeast chest from below. On the return, push the ball at (11,8) left; it drops into the lower route you have finished using. Its timing changes access to the southeast corner.
5. Work upward through the eastern room, collect the two eastern chests, and lift the left-arrow at (8,3) into row 2 to open the northern chest approach.
6. Take the chest under the ball at (6,2), then leave sideways. That ball drops to (6,3); the right-arrow at (5,3) rounds it into row 2 and releases the western gate ball from (4,3). The top chest then acts as a second stopper until collected.
7. Return to the same marshmallow parked at (8,6), approach it from above, and push it down to (8,7). This opens the westbound workspace route while the other central marshmallow remains parked above.
8. Keep the western marshmallow at (3,5) in place. Push the fallen gate ball at (4,6) left into (3,6), then descend onto the stairs. Moving that marshmallow down prematurely would jam this final operation.

## 2. The Last Cork

Stable ID: `challenge-c`. 15 × 13; 90 actions.

1. Raise the marshmallow only as far as (7,6), reserving it for permanent spike cover. Use the western loop to get above the southern cork.
2. Lower the southern cork to row 9, get to its left, and commit it to the southeast hole. The left recess is an unrecoverable parking trap.
3. Move the upper-right cork down into the shared row 5, then transport it left to (5,5). The northern cork must remain in place as temporary spike cover.
4. Advance the marshmallow to (7,5) while preserving access through the lower loop. Lift the westbound cork at column 5 and plug the western hole from its right.
5. Use the now-vacated eastern lifting space: move the marshmallow right into column 8, raise it to row 3, then push it left into (7,3). This replacement cover releases the northern cork for its final job.
6. Push the former shield cork right to column 9, then up into the northern hole. Collect its chest and return to the stairs.

## 3. Second Floor

Stable ID: `challenge-20261024-second-floor-v1`. 9 × 9; 42 actions.

2. Return through the middle to collect the chest, then stand just ahead of the arrow and step upward. The ball redirects the arrow diagonally into the next lane.
3. Approach the ball from the left and shift it right one square; push the arrow up twice so it flies into the central shaft.
4. Return below and push the ball farther right to reopen access beneath the arrow.
5. Push the arrow up twice in the shaft to shield the spike beside the starting area, then loop above it to the exit.

## 4. The Third Hand

Stable ID: `challenge-20261024-the-third-hand-v1`. 15 × 11; 103 actions.

1. Park the axe in the spike lane to reach the upper room and take the shears.
2. Cut the vine above the chest, then set the shears immediately left of that chest as an arrow catcher.
3. Collect the chest and step below it; circle around and push the caught arrow downward three times to shield the spike.
4. Recover the axe and clear the first tree, exchange for the shears to cut the middle vine, then recover the axe again.
5. Carve the second tree, push its canoe right and then upward to open the final approach.
6. Park the axe, return for the shears, and cut the last vine to collect the chest and reach the stairs.

## 5. Cellar Door

Stable ID: `challenge-20261030-cellar-door-v1`. 11 × 9; 23 actions.

1. From (4,3) push the marshmallow left to (2,3), then from (2,2) push it down into (2,4). It screens the crossing (3,4) from the upper spike and sits directly above the ball at (2,5).
2. Go to (6,1), beside the ball at (7,1), and step down to (6,2). The ball drops one square onto the ball at (7,3); with Blobbo standing on its left it slides down-right into the shaft at column 8.
3. Falling past (8,5) it wakes the arrow at (9,5) (the only way to release it: (8,4) is beside the right spike and the lane can only be entered next to the ball at (2,5)). The arrow flies left along row 5; it cannot slide over the ball at (2,5) because the marshmallow is above it, so it slides under, into (2,6), where the lower spike stops it.
4. Walk (3,3), (3,4), (3,5), (3,6) down to the stairs at (3,7), screened from the upper spike by the marshmallow and from the lower spike by the arrow.

## 6. The Doorman

Stable ID: `challenge-20261030-the-doorman-v1`. 9 × 9; 37 actions.

1. The stairs (1,4) sit directly below the spike (1,3), so they are unusable while it stays there. A spike only moves by charging, so it has to be baited.
2. Push the cork west along row 6 to (2,6), go below it and push it up three times: it plugs the hole (2,3) beside the spike. This deliberately opens the spike's view along row 3.
3. Leave both chests alone. Row 3 is now exposed except (7,3), which the chest at (6,3) still screens: cross upward there into the upper room.
4. Walk round to (5,2), right of the hanging ball, and step down into row 3. Leaving (5,2) drops the ball onto (4,3), where the pocket chest (4,4) holds it; the spike charges from (1,3) and stops at (3,3) against the ball.
5. With the ball screening row 3, collect the chest at (6,3). Return to (5,3), drop onto the one-way floor (5,4) and take the pocket chest (4,4); stepping back out lets the ball fall into the hole (4,5).
6. Leave the one-way floor downward and walk round the bottom to the stairs, which are now free.

## 8. The Lean-To

Stable ID: `challenge-20261030-the-lean-to-v1`. 11 × 8; 28 actions.

1. Climb to (5,4) in row 4 but no higher: every turn spent in the top row lets the vine at (1,2) grow toward (3,2), the stairs' only access square, and nothing will ever be able to wall it off in time.
2. Grow the row-4 vine from (1,4) until its tip is at (4,4), beside the gap: step right to (6,4) and wait exactly once (or stand at (5,4) and wait twice, where it stops by itself). Leave row 4 downward.
3. Walk to (9,6), step up to (9,5) under the arrow and back down. Leaving that square releases the arrow; it flies left and stops at (5,4), right in the gap, against the vine tip.
4. While the arrow sits at (5,4) it blocks the row-4 vine. Enter row 4 at (9,4), collect (7,3), and leave via (6,4)->(6,5)->(5,5).
5. Walk up into the arrow twice: the first push lifts it to (5,3), where a wall stops it; the second lifts it to (5,2), from where it flies left and stops at (2,2), walling off the stairs vine for good.
6. Climb into the top row, collect (5,1), walk to (3,2) and enter the stairs.

## 9. Top Shelf

Stable ID: `challenge-20261030-top-shelf-v1`. 11 × 8; 38 actions.

1. The stairs (1,4) sit directly below the spike (1,3), so the spike must be lured along row 3. The near ball (3,2) would park it at (2,3), beside the stairs' only entrance (1,3), so the far ball (7,2) has to be the brake. It parks the spike at (6,3), and from there the spike would see up column 6 and cut row 1, your only way back west. So column 6 must be capped first.
2. Push the cork up one square to (4,4) only, which frees (4,5). From (4,5) push the marshmallow east to (6,5), go below it and push it up three times to (6,2). The last push puts you on (6,3), which is safe only while the pit is still open.
3. Now finish the cork: push it from (4,4) into the pit (4,3). The spike's view along row 3 is open, and only (9,3) is still screened, by the chest (8,3). Leave that chest alone.
4. Walk round to (9,5), up through (9,3) to (9,2), left to (8,2), and step down onto the chest (8,3). Collecting it is the lure: the far ball drops onto (7,3) and the spike charges from (1,3) and stops at (6,3).
5. Go back up and west along row 1; the marshmallow at (6,2) blocks the spike's view up column 6. Step into (4,2) and back out so the near ball drops onto (3,3), screening (1,3).
6. Walk to (1,1) and down through (1,2) and (1,3) onto the stairs.

## 10. Tenth Frame

1. Take the top chest (5,1) and step back right. The ball at (4,1) drops to (4,3); the right ramp at (4,4) would throw it down-right into (5,4), but the chest there jams the ramp, so the ball rests at (4,3) and screens the spike row (3).

3. Push the marshmallow right twice, from (3,6) to (5,6). It will be the floor that stops the ball at (5,5).

The arrow flies left, slides over the ball at (6,6) into row 5, slides over the fallen ball at (5,5) into (5,4), and the ramp lifts it up-left into (4,3), where the spike stops it: the new shield.

## 11. Greenhouse Aisle

Stable ID: `challenge-20261030-greenhouse-aisle-v1`. 15 × 10; 41 actions.

1. Walk right along the bottom and climb the far-right shaft (12,7)->(12,3). This must be your first entry into the aisle (row 3): any step into row 3 from the left shaft lets the left-pointing vine at (13,3) grow into (12,3) and seal the right shaft for good.
2. Step left twice to (10,3), under the right ball. The right vine follows to (11,3) and stops, adjacent. Wait twice: the shaft vine at (10,7) climbs to (10,4).
3. Step left to (9,3): the ball drops into (10,3), lands on the shaft vine's tip and permanently walls off the right vine. Collect the chest at (9,2) (safe now that its ball has dropped).
4. Walk left to the axe at (3,3). Picking it up removes the only thing blocking the left vine, which grows to (2,3). Wait twice so the shaft vine at (3,7) climbs to (3,4), then step right: the left ball drops onto the axe's old square and replaces the axe as the left vine's wall.
5. Climb to (6,2), chop the palm at (7,2), push the canoe to (8,2) and collect (7,1).
6. Return to (5,3), descend the left shaft and step onto the stairs at (4,8).
