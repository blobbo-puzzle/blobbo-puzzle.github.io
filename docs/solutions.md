# Regular solutions — spoilers

Developer reference, excluded from the website and standalone HTML. U/D/L/R move, W waits, X drops the held tool. These are verified winning witnesses, not guaranteed shortest routes.

## 1. Borrowed Weight

Stable ID: `compact-borrowed-weight-v1`. 10 × 9; 16 actions.

1. Pick up the extinguisher and drop it below the hanging ball as a temporary support.
2. Release the ball onto that support, keeping the spike screened while collecting the other chest.
3. Push the ball onto the side ledge before retrieving the extinguisher. Clear the fire and descend to the exit.


</details>
